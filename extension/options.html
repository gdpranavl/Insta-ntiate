Insta-ntiate · Collector settings

This collector runs in your own browser session. No passwords leave your machine.

1 · Connection

Where to push synced archives. Default: the local Next app on port 3000. Change this if you host the app somewhere else.

2 · Account

Leave the username blank and tick auto-detect to pull it from your browser session. Manual override wins if both are set.

3 · What to sync

Pick which saved collections to archive. Default is "everything you have saved."

4 · Schedule

Runs while Chrome is open. Browser may delay alarms on battery.
